Delete Your Goreto Account

Applies to the Goreto app (developer: Goreto, package com.nex.ekloapp)

Option 1: Delete from within the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Goreto app and log in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the request

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't log in, email help@goreto.org from the address associated with your account (or include your registered phone number/username) with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify your identity and process the request manually.

What happens after you request deletion

  • Your account is scheduled for deletion with a 30-day grace period — if you log back in during this window, deletion is automatically cancelled and your account is restored.
  • After 30 days, your profile, posts, stories, photos/videos, and chat messages are permanently deleted.

What data is deleted vs. retained

In line with our Privacy Policy:

  • Deleted within 30 days: profile information, posts, stories, photos/videos, bio, chat messages, follows/blocks, location data
  • Retained up to 5 years (legal/financial compliance): wallet transaction history and KYC identity verification records
  • Retained up to 90 days: security/access log data
Any coin balance remaining at the time of deletion is forfeited and cannot be refunded, consistent with our Terms & Conditions.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific data. From within the app, while your account stays active, you can:

  • Delete a post, reel, or story: open the post → tap the ⋯ menu → Delete
  • Remove your profile photo or cover photo: Profile → Edit Profile → tap the photo → Remove
  • Clear your bio, location, or other profile details: Profile → Edit Profile → clear the field → Save
  • Delete a chat conversation: open the chat → tap ⋯ → Delete Conversation

Deleted posts, photos, and messages are removed immediately and are not recoverable. This does not affect wallet transaction history or KYC verification records, which are retained separately as described above for legal/financial compliance. To request deletion of any other specific data not listed here, email help@goreto.org describing what you'd like removed — we respond within 30 days.
